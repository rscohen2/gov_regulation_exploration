cfr correction in title 12 of the code of federal regulations, parts 1026 to 1099, revised as of january 1, 2016, on page 749, in supplement i to part 1026, under section 1026.41,the heading supplement i to part 1026—official interpretation ii. the periodic statement is not required for any portion of the mortgage debt that is discharged under applicable provisions of the u.s. bankruptcy code. if the consumer's bankruptcy case is revived—for example if the court reinstates a previously dismissed case, reopens the case, or revokes a discharge—the servicer is again exempt from the requirement in § 1026.41.
